Narrative
A 65-year-old Asian woman with a 10-year history of essential hypertension, well controlled until these events. Her primary care physician started DrugX 10 mg once daily on 2025-11-01. She had previously been managed with lifestyle changes alone and had never taken antihypertensive medication. Baseline blood pressure at prescription was 152/94 mmHg.

Three days after the first dose (2025-11-04) she developed a severe, throbbing frontal headache with dizziness on standing. The next day she also had an episode of nausea without vomiting. She did not seek care immediately; she contacted the prescriber on 2025-11-14, and DrugX was stopped the same day. No symptomatic treatment was needed. At the time of this report, all three symptoms had been gradually improving over about 3 days after discontinuation.

No other cause was identified. The patient reported no recent change in diet, no new medicines and no intercurrent illness. The temporal relationship between starting the drug and the onset of symptoms supports a causal relationship with DrugX assessed as possible.

Follow-up
Follow-up is planned at the next scheduled visit on 2025-12-01 to confirm full recovery.
